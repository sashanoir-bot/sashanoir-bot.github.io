Privacy Policy

Application: Sasha Drive

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Purpose

Sasha Drive is a private integration that uses Google Drive to back up and restore data belonging to the Sasha Noir AI agent project.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive access to create, read, update, and delete files required for backup and restoration operations.

Data storage

Project backups are stored in the authorized Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally and are not published on this website.

Data sharing

The integration is intended for private use. It does not sell user data or intentionally share Google Drive data with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections settings.
